# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Changed

- **Athlete career.** From 16, choose football, basketball or tennis in Career, Athlete. Football and basketball: trial for one of eight fictional clubs (a contract is a paid job, two years, renewed if you earn a place), get picked by fitness, form and readiness (with the reason when you are left out), and play an eight-month season with a live league table; basketball adds a top-four playoff and has no draws. Tennis: no club, a tournament each month with prize money by round and a world ranking. Each month choose training (technical, tactical, recovery) and how you play key moments; seasons and titles go on your record. Professional athlete jobs are no longer on the job board.

- **Music and acting rebalanced.** Released songs stay in your song list (marked released) instead of vanishing; you can write three sessions a month, on the same song if you like, and songs come together faster; royalties pay about three times more and new fans arrive faster, so a label and bigger venues are within reach. Acting pay now grows with your recognition and reputation (up to four times as much for a famous, respected actor); a film pays its whole fee as one lump sum when filming wraps; a TV season is 12 episodes, paid month by month.

- **Children raised off-screen learn too.** Children born during play kept every skill at 0 until you played them, because only the character you play learnt from school. They now catch up once a school year (up to 26) to a level that fits their age, and anyone you take over (a child or an heir) catches up at that moment, so older saves are fixed when you switch.

- **Overdue bills are paid from savings too.** Overdue bills used to be paid only from the current account, so money arriving in savings (birthday money, interest) sat idle while the 1% monthly late fee grew. They are now paid from the current account, then savings, and Money explains what overdue bills are.

- **Job board:** actor, musician and film director roles are only started from Career, Paths (no longer advertised in Job App), and the board is fuller: 6 to 10 new jobs a month, up to 50 listed. Transactions now read "Royalties" for both book and music royalties (one label used to overwrite the other).

- **Doctors: registration and a patient queue.** Practising as a doctor now needs a medical degree and registration: work as a resident doctor for your country's supervised months (routine patients only), then pass the registration exam. Nursing and other health roles see why they cannot take a doctor's cases. Registered doctors get a patient queue in Career, Patients: review information cards, talk with the patient and consult a colleague, then arrange follow-up care or refer. Cases are fictional and non-graphic, with no diagnoses, medicines or procedures.

- **Journalists: credential and story desk.** The journalist role now needs a relevant (media) diploma or degree; unrelated or unfinished study does not count. Journalists get a story desk in Career, Stories: take a story, verify claims, interview sources and draft, then publish a short report or an in-depth feature. Publishing with unverified or disputed claims brings a correction and costs integrity; verified reporting builds it.

- **Lawyers: admission and cases.** Practising as a lawyer now needs a law degree and admission: work as a trainee lawyer for your country's supervised months, then pass the licensing exam (a law diploma leads to paralegal work, never practice; a serious unspent conviction suspends it). Admitted lawyers get a case board in Career, Cases: take a client's case, examine the evidence, talk to the client and prepare, then settle or go to a hearing; the result and its main reason are explained and kept in your career record.

- **Business: market research.** Commission a customer survey (10% of the local median wage, paid by the company, once a month) for a likely demand range next month at your price and at 10% cheaper or dearer, next to what your free staff can serve.

- **Business: client contracts.** Consultancies, software studios and contractors now win client briefs: sign with a lean or thorough scope, put staff on the project (they leave walk-in work), get 30% up front and the rest on delivery less 10% for each month late. Happy clients come back with bigger briefs; work three months late is cancelled. A hired manager signs fitting work for you.

- **Music career:** musicians write original songs over several sessions (alone or co-writing with another musician for half the credit), record them at a home, independent or professional studio, and release singles, EPs or albums independently or through a label (advance, recoupment, royalty share, releases owed, exclusive). Releases earn capped monthly royalties while they last, compete on a monthly chart with fictional artists and can win a Golden Note; live shows come from a venue board that grows with your fans. An optional simulated mode runs routine music work each month (Career, Music).

- **Acting: industry outlook.** One genre is in fashion each year: more castings come in it and its releases draw bigger audiences, but fashion changes yearly.

- **Acting: typecasting and legacy.** With three or more credits mostly in one genre, casting knows you for it: those parts are easier to win, others harder. A person's filmography and awards show on their profile, also after they die.

- **Acting career:** actors get a Casting board (film and TV castings with studio, director, character, script quality, pay and deadline), audition with an approach and get honest feedback, accept, negotiate or decline offers, then work through rehearsals, filming and post-production. Pay arrives at milestones less an agent's commission; films are reviewed by critics and audiences, TV seasons are renewed or cancelled, strong work can win a Silver Reel, and everything lands in your Filmography (Career, Acting).

- **Lottery and casino:** adults can buy lottery tickets (jackpot one in ten million) and bet on red at roulette, from Money, Investments. Both lose money on average.

- **Prices stay in today's money:** wages, house and car prices no longer multiply over the generations; only booms, slumps and policies move them, and interest rates are real rates. Saves already far in the future get a one-time currency reform on the next Advance, so they read in today's money too. A family can now give money from savings when its current account is empty.

- **Month recap:** after Advance you see what changed: your stats, money in and out, and what happened. Five tabs now (Life, Career, Social, Money, More) and one wide Advance button.

- **New look, step 1:** the Life screen has a compact character header with your current account and a list of colourful action rows; choose a game colour (Jade, Grape, Guava or Ocean) in Settings.

- **Take over a child:** Legacy, Hand over now lists your children and grandchildren of any age, so you can carry on as a young child while your character lives on.

- **Long-term conditions:** asthma and other chronic conditions now flare up some months instead of draining health every month, and can be cured with an expensive therapy or operation from the Health care screen.

- **Vehicles:** cars, planes and helicopters now lose value each month and cost money to run; sell them from Shopping for what they are worth now.

- **Hair fix:** new characters (you, your family and everyone you meet) start with a hairstyle typical for their gender; boys no longer get long straight hair, a bob or a ponytail by default. Existing characters keep their hair; visit the barber to change it.
- **Barber or salon:** a new "Look & style" category in the Activities Hub. Pick any of eight hairstyles with a live preview, and from age 14 a new colour; paid through your accounts (or by your family while you are a child), once a month.
- **Finding a partner:** single adults get a Dating card on the Social tab, listing single people their age they know, how close they are, and how much closer they need to be to ask them on a date. "Look for love" (18+, single) introduces someone single who is interested in you. People you are getting close to no longer pair off with someone else meanwhile, and "Ask on a date" says exactly how much closer you need to be. "Meet new people" now names who you met.
- **Clear feedback:** what happens when you do something now appears in a pop-up near the bottom of the screen (above Advance on phones), so you see it wherever you have scrolled. If something cannot be done (not enough money, not close enough, too young, no time left), the pop-up is red and says why. Locked buttons can be tapped to explain why instead of doing nothing.
- **Rival job offers:** while you have a job, a rival company may approach you with the same kind of role (sometimes a level up) for 10-30% more pay. It is likelier with a strong reputation, a specialism and good work (1-8% a month; never in your first six months, as an intern or casual worker, or more than once a year). Accept, negotiate, decline, or ask your current employer to match it (the chance is shown; if they refuse, the offer stays open). Advancing several months now also stops when any job offer arrives.
- **Taking money out of your retirement fund:** the Retirement card on the Finance tab now explains that the fund pays a monthly income once you retire (and shows about how much), and from age 55 you can take 25% of it as cash once in your life, tax-free.
- **Clearer monthly money:** Money this month now shows the debt you repaid on each mortgage or loan (the part beyond interest), with Money in, Money out and the true net for the month. Before, a mortgage payment only counted its interest.
- **Musicians have their own career:** instead of auditioning for film and TV, musicians play paid gigs (better paid as their reputation grows) and release singles; a hit pays royalties every month for half a year.
- **Choose a path:** the Career tab now has the Job App in the middle and four paths at the bottom: Army (enlist), and start freelancing as a Musician, Actor or Film director once your skills are good enough. Directors shoot short films for credits, reputation and festival prizes.
- **Moving fixes:** after moving country the job board shows local jobs straight away (old jobs from your previous town no longer appear with the wrong currency). Moving into a home you own abroad now says to move country first, instead of leaving you living there on your old currency. Finance shows your own net worth; the family total moved to Legacy as Family wealth.
- **Business products:** shops, cafés and other goods businesses can launch up to four extra products, each with its own price, supplier, stock level and advertising budget. After each month you see units sold, stock, costs, ad spend and profit per product, and you can retune or drop a product that loses money.
- **Acting contracts:** a successful audition now lands a film (fee up front, two-month shoot) or a TV series (paid monthly for three months). Screen credits move you from extra to supporting roles to leads, and the Career card shows your credits and current production.
- **Military roles:** volunteers choose a specialty (logistics, engineering, medical support or administration), which trains a skill civilian employers value, and a home or overseas posting. Overseas pays more and helps promotion but adds stress and costs family time. Your service shows the current promotion chance.
- **Volunteering builds a political track record:** volunteer at a food bank, help at a community clinic or organise a neighbourhood clean-up (Activities, Social). Each raises your community standing, shown on the Politics screen, which gives your next campaign a head start.
- **Job board filters:** filter openings by monthly pay range, field of study, industry and contract type, or show only jobs you qualify for. Jobs you can apply for are listed first, best paid first.
- **Government bonds:** lend money to the government for 1, 5 or 10 years at a fixed rate (longer pays more). Interest arrives every month and your money comes back at the end; selling early pays the current price, which falls when interest rates rise and rises when they fall.
- **Investment gains:** each fund now shows how much you put in and how much you are up or down.
- **Gender options:** a new life is now a girl, a boy or random (the non-binary option was removed from New Life; existing saves are unaffected).

- **New Life tab header:** a large portrait, your name, age, what you are doing and where you live, with four headline stats (health, happiness, intelligence, energy). On this tab the top bar shows just the month. The old Profile and Vitals sections start hidden; turn them back on with Customise.
- **"What do you want to do?" grid:** Activities, School or Work, People, Money, Calendar and More.
- **Redesigned profile:** portrait banner, Generation and life-stage badges, personal details with icons, and key statistics.
- **Clearer decisions:** each decision shows an icon for its kind of event, larger choice cards, and a closed choice now says why it is closed.
- **GitHub Pages:** the game is published at https://razinraziq13.github.io/welife/.

### Added

- **Your story:** the Life tab shows the three latest moments of your life, with a link to the whole story.
- **Achievement pop-ups:** a small, dismissible note when an achievement unlocks, with a link to the collection. Achievements already unlocked when a life is opened are not announced again.
- **Life summary:** when a character dies, their life gets a title (for example "The Explorer" or "The Family Anchor"), a few facts and up to five defining moments, shown above the heir choice. Past players' titles also appear in the family line.
- **Roadmap:** `docs/ROADMAP.md` lists what comes next.
- **Deeper interactions:** 15 new things to do with people, grouped into Talk, Friendship, Family, Romance and Work: share a secret, ask a favour, gossip, make peace, talk it through, ask family for money or permission, host a family get-together, support a relative, make something with a friend, talk about the future with a partner, and at work negotiate your salary, ask for a promotion, network or find a mentor. Outcomes depend on the other person and are never guaranteed; each unavailable option says why.
- **Careers with their own play:** teachers, doctors and nurses, performers and office workers each get their own work actions (lessons, extra shifts paid as overtime, complex cases, auditions with paid roles, projects, paid courses). Professional reputation follows you between jobs and helps at review time; a specialism raises pay by 10%; job satisfaction (and what drives it) affects your happiness. Eight new work decisions: credit taken, leading a project, disagreements, a restless class, covering a night shift, a difficult case, a lead role, being headhunted.
- **Activities Hub:** one searchable place for everything you can do with free time, in nine categories, with costs, favourites, monthly plans and suggestions with reasons. 25 new activities, including doctor and dentist visits, cinema, concerts and museums, day trips and holidays abroad, shopping, family dinners, parties, a driving test, and personal projects that take months (write a novel, build an app, learn a language, grow a garden) and can end in published books or app income.

## 0.9.0 (release candidate, 2026-11-19)

Milestone 9: integration, balance and release preparation.

### Added

- **Royal and noble succession.** A house has a head, separate from inheritance and from who you play. The headship passes by each country's rule (agnatic, male preference or absolute), and the Legacy screen shows the house, its rule, the line of succession and past heads.
- **Long-run stability.** People who have left your life keep ageing off screen and eventually die. Once a year, people who died long ago and matter to no lasting record are cleaned up, and their closed books move into an archive account. A 10,000-month soak (`npm run soak`) checks that the world stays bounded and valid across 10+ generations.
- **Cross-browser determinism test.** A save built in Node is played in the browser and must match a Node replay exactly.
- **Security.** Production builds ship a Content Security Policy; see `docs/SECURITY.md`.
- **Translatable refusals.** Every refused action carries a translation key, with English text from one table.

### Changed

- **Advance plays four months per click** and stops early for a decision or the end of a life (decision D11).
- **Difficulty affects business rivals** (weaker on Casual, stronger on Challenging).
- **Former players keep paying loans and earning interest** after you switch to an heir.
- **Faster first load.** Screens load on demand: the main script is 271 KB instead of 991 KB, and the service worker still caches everything for offline play.
- When someone who ran a household budget dies, the household gets its own accounts and a new head, instead of paying bills from a closed account.

### Known limits

- 131 built-in events against the 400+ target (D8).
- Tax, wage and school figures are baseline approximations that have not been checked against official sources.
- Installing on a physical phone or tablet still needs a manual check.
