# What WeLife can learn from BitLife

_Summary, 2026-11-19. BitLife details come from general knowledge of the game, not a fresh review._

## In one sentence

WeLife is deeper than BitLife (real countries, a real ledger, generations, rewind, an event editor), but BitLife is faster, funnier and has far more to do. The gap is mostly **pace, tone and content**, not the engine.

## Where each game is stronger

| BitLife is stronger                                              | WeLife is stronger                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One tap = one year; a life takes minutes                         | Real double-entry money, 20 currencies, loans, tax, investments |
| Humorous, shareable moments                                      | 20 countries with real laws, visas, health care and schools     |
| Thousands of short scenarios (WeLife: 131)                       | Family lines, estates, wills, royal succession                  |
| One-tap activities: gym, doctor, love, holidays, lottery, casino | Rewind and branch timelines, free                               |
| Pets                                                             | Event editor and content packs                                  |
| Crime, prison and escapes                                        | Fully offline: no ads, no account, no tracking                  |
| Special careers (acting, music, sport, astronaut)                | Accessible (screen readers, keyboard, themes) and deterministic |
| End-of-life summary with a fun label; weekly challenges          |                                                                 |

## Improvements, in priority order

| #   | Improvement                | What it means                                                                                        | Effort |
| --- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| 1   | **Faster play**            | A "next year" button (12 months, stops on decisions) and a simple mode that hides the deeper screens | Small  |
| 2   | **More content, more fun** | 270+ new events to reach 400+, with humour and memorable moments, not only realistic ones            | Large  |
| 3   | **Life summary at death**  | A "how this life went" screen with a playful label, key stats and a shareable card                   | Small  |
| 4   | **Pets**                   | Adopt, care for and lose pets; they join the family records                                          | Medium |
| 5   | **Quick actions**          | One-tap doctor, gym, holiday, night out, lottery; casino behind an 18+ gate                          | Medium |
| 6   | **Crime and prison**       | Abstract choices with risk, sentences and prison life, built on the existing law system; no how-to   | Medium |
| 7   | **Fame careers**           | Acting, music and sport paths linked to the existing social media                                    | Medium |
| 8   | **Weekly challenges**      | Goals like "a millionaire before 30 in Japan", seeded by the week so they work offline               | Small  |
| 9   | **Mini-games**             | A few short ones (exam, job interview, court case) to break up the text                              | Medium |

**Suggested first step:** 1 and 3 together (quick wins that change how the game feels), then the content push (2), which is also the main item left before version 1.0.0.

## Keep what makes WeLife different

Real countries, the balanced ledger, generations, rewind, the editor, offline privacy and accessibility. The goal is BitLife's speed and fun on top of WeLife's depth, with **original** content: no copied scenarios, names, art or wording from BitLife.
